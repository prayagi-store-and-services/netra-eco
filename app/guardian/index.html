Release information

v1.0.0: first standalone Guardian release. Home-screen widget, Hindi and English labels, media-aware voice gate and approximate observed-session estimates. Optional cooling brightness consent starts OFF.

Physical-phone widget resizing, voice routing and document-picker acceptance are not yet verified. The signing certificate may differ from an earlier development build; Android may require uninstalling that build, which can remove local data. No cross-app history import is claimed.
