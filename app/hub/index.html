Android safety dashboard with sensor tools, SOS, earthquake information and official IMD subdivision / NDMA CAP reports matched on the phone. Green reports stay silent; verified Yellow, Orange and Red IMD warnings can notify and speak. Checks are about 30 minutes, subject to Android delays, not every forecast or district nowcast. Beta/testing, not an emergency-service replacement; physical-phone delivery unverified. Updates through Netra Ecosystem only.
