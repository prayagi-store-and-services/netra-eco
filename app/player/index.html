Release information

v1.1.21: optional usage reporting requires explicit opt-in. Track-dialog lifecycle, audio-only labels, About and release metadata were checked with automated tests and rendered emulator screens.

Crash reports show their text and destination before a user-tap send through FormSubmit HTTPS. File-level end-to-end encryption is not implemented. Physical-phone, TV, Bluetooth and battery acceptance have not been verified.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. You can see your Android version under Settings > About phone before downloading. The download is only an APK file: whether your phone can run it, and the decision to install it, remain your responsibility.

Android only. On an Android phone the Open app button launches the app if it is installed; if Android cannot find it, you stay on this page to download it.
