Netra Hub parking spot - field test checklist

Status: NOT yet verified on a phone. Hub 1.4.28 only passed unit tests and CI. This page is for the owner's real drive test. Do not trust the parking spot until these pass.

What this tests

Since 1.4.28 the spot is the middle of the GPS fixes where the phone stayed under 3 km/h for at least 10 seconds after driving, using only fixes accurate to 50 m or better. A confirmed stop is kept while you walk away. With no confirmed stop nothing is saved, the earlier spot stays, and the screen says why.

How to measure

  1. After parking, open Maps and drop a pin on the vehicle.
  2. In Hub open SOS and crash alert settings, press Open in Maps for the saved spot.
  3. Write the distance in metres between the two pins, the accuracy shown, and the time shown.
