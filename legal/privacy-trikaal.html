Privacy policy - Netra Trikaal

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Developer contact: prayagideepak@gmail.com.

The app, in short

Netra Trikaal provides panchang, kundli and astrology information. The birth details and places you enter stay on your phone.

The app does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Permissions

Coarse location, only to calculate timings for your place. Notifications. Network and install-permission for update checks.

Network use

This app does not use the anonymous usage counter and does not send crash reports. Its only network use is checking for app updates, which sends no personal data.

Contact

To ask about, correct or delete something, email prayagideepak@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
