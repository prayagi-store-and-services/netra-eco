Privacy policy - Netra Charge

Last updated: October 25, 2026. Developer contact: prayagideepak@gmail.com.

The app, in short

Netra Charge speaks four battery and charger announcements: Connected, Disconnected, Full charge and Low battery. The low-battery threshold you choose (15% by default) is stored privately on your phone. The app does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

What the app reads on your phone

To decide when to speak, the app reads the battery and charger state, whether the screen is on, and whether media is playing. These are processed in memory on the phone at that moment; the app does not store them and does not upload them.

Permissions

Foreground service (special use), so the charging announcer can keep running in the background, and Notifications, so the announcer can show its status. The app does not request the internet, location, contacts, microphone or phone-call permissions.

Network use

The app does not request the internet permission and its code contains no network, analytics or upload calls: no update checks, no usage counters, no crash reports, no account. This app also does not use the anonymous usage counter described on the Data safety page.

Spoken announcements

The announcement words are spoken by the Android text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your phone. That engine is not part of Netra Charge. Which voices are available, and whether a voice works offline, depends on your phone's TTS engine and its voice data, which are outside the app's control.

Android backup

The app's manifest disables Android app backup (android:allowBackup="false").

Website downloads

The Netra Ecosystem website counts visits and downloads with its own anonymous increment-only counter, as described in the website section of the Data safety page. This is website activity, separate from the app itself.

Contact

To ask about, correct or delete something, email prayagideepak@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
