Data safety declarations

Last updated: October 25, 2026. What data each Netra app handles, in the style of an app-store data-safety form. The apps are distributed directly from this website, not through an app store; this page is our own disclosure, not a store certification.

Common to all apps

  • No account, no ads, no data sale.
  • Anonymous usage counter (optional, where listed): at most once per day and once per month the app increments a public counter in Google Firebase. The request carries no device ID, install ID, account, location or app data, and can be turned off in the app's Settings.
  • Update checks: each app checks its public GitHub releases page for new versions, sending no personal data.
  • Crash reports (where listed): saved on the phone after a crash and sent only when you review the exact text in the app and tap Send; they hold only app name, phone model, Android version, app version, crash time and the stack trace (exception messages dropped), and go to the developer's email inbox through the FormSubmit service.
  • Website: visits and downloads are counted anonymously (an increment-only counter on our own Firebase project; no cookies, no personal data). Messages sent through the Contact Us form are delivered to the developer by email.

Website ratings and feedback

The embedded Netra feedback form saves entries in publicly readable records with the phone model and any optional name or area entered. The ratings list displays the name (or Anonymous), phone model, optional area, star rating and optional comment. The feedback list displays Suggestion or Complaint, phone model, optional area and the required message; it does not display the saved name. These entries help answer feedback and improve the app. A copy of the form is emailed to the developer through FormSubmit. Do not include private documents, passwords or sensitive information. Sending is optional.
