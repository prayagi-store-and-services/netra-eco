Privacy policy - Netra Hub

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Developer contact: prayagideepak@gmail.com.

The app, in short

Netra Hub shows your phone's sensors and device status and runs alert features you switch on. What the app reads stays on your phone except the items listed below.

The app does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Location (including background, only if you grant it): location-based alerts and the earthquake feature.
  • Camera: the camera-check feature only.
  • SMS: the SOS feature sends a message only to emergency contacts you add yourself, and only when you enable and trigger it.
  • Activity recognition, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi state, usage access, notifications, exact alarms, battery-optimisation exemption: the related monitoring and alert features.

You can revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature then stops.

What leaves the phone

  • Earthquake feature: when used, the app sends your current location coordinates to the USGS earthquake API (earthquake.usgs.gov) to find recent quakes near you.
  • Public alert feed: the app downloads the public NDMA disaster-alert feed (sachet.ndma.gov.in). No personal data is sent.
  • Maps: map links open Google Maps in your browser or maps app.

Anonymous usage counter (optional)

At most once per day (and once per month) the app adds +1 to a public counter in Google Firebase so the team can see how many phones used the app. The request carries no device ID, install ID, account, location or app data - only "increment this counter by 1". It is on by default and can be turned off in the app's Settings.

Crash reports (automatic)

If the app crashes, a short report is saved on the phone and sent by itself the next time the app opens (then deleted from the phone). The report holds only: app name, phone model, Android version, app version, the time of the crash, and the crash stack trace (code locations; exception messages are dropped). It has no name, email, location, files or device IDs. It is delivered to the developer's email inbox through the FormSubmit service and used only to fix bugs. To ask for a report to be deleted, email prayagideepak@gmail.com.

Update checks

The app checks its public GitHub releases page for new versions. This sends no personal data.

Contact

To ask about, correct or delete something, email prayagideepak@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
