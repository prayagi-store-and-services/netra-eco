Privacy policy - Netra Privacy Guard

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Developer contact: prayagideepak@gmail.com.

The app, in short

Netra Privacy Guard gives you microphone and camera transparency and privacy-monitoring tools. The analysis runs on your phone.

The app does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Microphone: the microphone-transparency feature you switch on. The app does not send audio anywhere.
  • Device admin, usage access, app-list query, phone state, notifications, quick-settings tile and local filtering permissions: the protection features you enable. They work on your phone.

You can revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature then stops.

What leaves the phone

The app downloads a blocklist file from our public GitHub releases for its filtering features. No personal data is sent with that download.

Anonymous usage counter (optional)

At most once per day (and once per month) the app adds +1 to a public counter in Google Firebase so the team can see how many phones used the app. The request carries no device ID, install ID, account, location or app data - only "increment this counter by 1". It is on by default and can be turned off in the app's Settings.

Crash reports (your choice)

If the app crashes, a short report is saved on the phone. It is not sent by itself. To share it, open the app's Crash report card: you see the exact text first, and it is sent only when you tap Send. The report holds only: app name, phone model, Android version, app version, the time of the crash, and the crash stack trace (code locations; exception messages are dropped). It has no name, email, location, files or device IDs. It goes to the developer's email inbox through the FormSubmit service and is used only to fix bugs. To ask for a report to be deleted, email prayagideepak@gmail.com.

Update checks

The app checks its public GitHub releases page for new versions. This sends no personal data.

Contact

To ask about, correct or delete something, email prayagideepak@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
