Netra Hub driving detection - tester index

Facts below come from the owner's ride report and a code audit on 21 Oct 2026. No real ride has been done with the fix yet, so nothing here proves it works.

What failed (owner report, 21 Oct 2026, riding a two-wheeler with helmet and earphones, speed varying)

  1. Driving was not detected.
  2. No announcement said whether driving mode turned on.
  3. No detection record or log was visible in the app.
  4. The alert system (SOS and crash alert) was not easy to find.

Likely causes found in the code (Hub up to v1.4.23)

  • Driving mode only started when GPS speed reached 30 km/h, and ended after 30 seconds below it. City riding often stays under 30.
  • Location used balanced power with updates every 10 seconds. Many phones send positions without a speed in that mode, so speed read "unavailable" and driving mode never started.
  • The only driving state was a yes/no value in memory. Nothing was announced and nothing was logged. The card showed only while driving.
  • One Home card said "Drive detection is not available in this version" even though speed-based detection exists.
  • The SOS and crash alert setup was behind a small text button in the header.

Change being prepared (Hub v1.4.24, not released yet)

  • Start at 15 km/h, end after 90 seconds below.
  • High-accuracy GPS every 5 seconds; speed worked out from two fixes when the phone gives none.
  • Home "Driving detection" card: mode, GPS speed and its age, spoken announcement switch, TEST announcement button, driving log (time, speed, reason), and a button to the SOS and crash alert settings.
  • Known cost: high-accuracy GPS uses more battery.
